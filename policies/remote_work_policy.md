# Acme Corp - Remote & Hybrid Work Policy

## 1. Overview
Acme Corp operates a flexible hybrid working framework designed to support collaboration while providing individual focus time and autonomy.

---

## 2. Eligibility and Working Models
- **Hybrid Model (Default)**: Employees are expected to work from an Acme Corp hub or office at least **2 days per week** (typically Tuesday and Thursday for team synchronization).
- **Full-Remote Track**: Employees may apply for full-remote status if their role allows and upon written approval from their department director and People Operations.
- **Temporary Remote Work ("Work from Anywhere")**: Employees in good standing may work from another domestic or international location for up to **30 calendar days per year**, provided they remain tax-compliant and overlap with core hours.

---

## 3. Core Collaboration Hours
- Regardless of work location, all employees must be available and responsive on Slack and email during core collaboration hours: **10:00 AM – 4:00 PM (in the employee's local hub time zone)**.

---

## 4. Equipment & Home Office Stipends
- **Hardware Provided by Company**:
  - 1x Standard Company Laptop (MacBook Pro or Dell XPS).
  - Necessary security dongles and power accessories.
- **One-Time Home Office Stipend**:
  - Eligible employees receive a **\$500 one-time reimbursement** to purchase ergonomic office furniture, external monitors, keyboards, mice, or desks.
  - Expense receipts must be submitted via Concur/Expensify within **90 days** of employment start date.
- **Monthly Internet / Connectivity Allowance**:
  - All active hybrid and remote employees receive a recurring **\$50/month tax-free reimbursement** for high-speed home internet and mobile data.

---

## 5. Information Security & Data Protection
- Employees working remotely must connect via the Acme Corp Zero-Trust Corporate VPN when accessing sensitive databases or internal customer information.
- Use of unauthorized personal cloud accounts for company data is strictly prohibited.
