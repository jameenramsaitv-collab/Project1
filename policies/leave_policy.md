# Acme Corp - Employee Leave & Time-Off Policy

## 1. Overview
Acme Corp recognizes the importance of work-life harmony and personal well-being. This policy sets out the entitlements, rules, and procedures for taking paid and unpaid leave.

---

## 2. Paid Time Off (PTO) / Annual Leave
- **Accrual Rate**: Full-time employees accrue **20 days** of paid annual leave per calendar year (accrued at ~1.67 days per month worked).
- **Probationary Period**: New hires may access accrued PTO after completing their first 30 days of employment.
- **Carryover Limit**: A maximum of **5 unused PTO days** can be rolled over into the following calendar year. Any additional unused days expire on March 31st unless an exception is approved by HR.
- **Advance Notice**: For planned leave of 3 or more consecutive days, employees should submit requests through the HR portal at least 2 weeks in advance.

---

## 3. Sick & Medical Leave
- **Entitlement**: Full-time employees receive **10 paid sick days** per calendar year, granted on January 1st (or pro-rated for new hires).
- **Permissible Use**: Sick leave covers illness, medical appointments, recovery from injury, or caring for an immediate family member who is ill.
- **Documentation**: A valid doctor's note or medical certificate is required for absences extending beyond **3 consecutive business days**.
- **Unused Sick Leave**: Sick days do not roll over to the next year and are not paid out upon separation of employment.

---

## 4. Parental Leave
Acme Corp supports growing families through generous paid parental leave regardless of gender:
- **Primary Caregiver Leave**: **16 weeks** of 100% paid leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Secondary Caregiver Leave**: **6 weeks** of 100% paid leave following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child.
- **Timing**: Parental leave may be taken continuously or in two separate blocks within **12 months** of the child's arrival.
- **Reintegration**: Employees returning from parental leave are eligible for a 4-week "Gradual Return" schedule (working 80% hours at 100% pay upon manager agreement).

---

## 5. Bereavement Leave
- Employees are eligible for up to **5 consecutive business days** of fully paid leave in the event of the loss of an immediate family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling).
- Up to **3 consecutive business days** are provided for extended family members (grandparents, in-laws, aunts/uncles).

---

## 6. How to Request Leave
1. Log in to the HR Portal at `https://hr.acmecorp.internal/leave`.
2. Select the leave category, date range, and optional notes.
3. Submit for direct manager approval. Managers are expected to respond within 48 hours.
