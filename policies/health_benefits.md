# Acme Corp - Employee Health, Medical & Wellness Benefits

## 1. Overview
Acme Corp provides comprehensive medical, dental, vision, and mental health coverage to support the health and well-being of employees and their eligible dependents.

---

## 2. Medical Insurance Plans
Employees may choose between two health plan options starting on the first day of the calendar month following their start date:
- **Acme Comprehensive PPO**:
  - Low in-network deductible (\$500 individual / \$1,000 family).
  - Copays: \$20 for primary care visits, \$40 for specialists.
  - Acme covers **85% of employee monthly premiums** and **70% of dependent premiums**.
- **High-Deductible Health Plan (HDHP) with HSA**:
  - Higher deductible with eligibility for Health Savings Account (HSA).
  - Acme contributes **\$1,000 annually (individual)** or **\$2,000 annually (family)** into your HSA.

---

## 3. Dental & Vision Coverage
- **Dental**: 100% covered preventative care (two cleanings and check-ups per year). Major dental work covered at 80% up to \$2,000 annual maximum.
- **Vision**: Annual eye exam 100% covered. Up to **\$250 annual frame or contact lens stipend**.

---

## 4. Mental Health & Wellness
- **Employee Assistance Program (EAP)**: All employees have access to **10 confidential therapy or counseling sessions per calendar year** at zero cost through our partner network (Modern Health).
- **Annual Wellness Reimbursement**: Up to **\$400 per calendar year** for gym memberships, fitness classes, meditation apps, or athletic equipment.

---

## 5. Filing Claims & Inquiries
- **Benefits Portal**: Manage enrollments, view policy documents, and update dependents at `https://benefits.acmecorp.internal`.
- **Claim Reimbursement**: For out-of-network claims, submit itemized receipts through the portal within **90 days** of receiving medical care.
- **HR Benefits Desk**: Email `benefits-support@acmecorp.internal` for enrollment support or urgent questions.
